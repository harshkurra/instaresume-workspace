# Website Business Strategy

Quick-launch service business: build simple professional websites for Indian SMEs and professionals, with a custom WhatsApp widget. Zero platform cost to start, diaspora expansion in month 2-3.

---

## The Model

**What you sell:** A clean 5-section professional website + custom WhatsApp button, built and managed by you.

**Pricing:**
- Setup: ₹2,999 (one-time)
- Hosting/maintenance: ₹699/month
- Domain: client pays ~₹300/year directly to Namecheap (not your cost)

**Your cost per client: ₹0** (Vercel free tier, Sanity free tier, your own template)

---

## Target Market

### Phase 1 — India (Month 1-2)
**Best segment: Professionals** — they pay without negotiating, have high income, and need to look credible online (patients/clients Google them before visiting).

| Vertical | Pitch | Best sub-targets |
|---|---|---|
| Dentists | "Patients Google dentists before booking" | New clinics in residential colonies |
| Skin/cosmetic clinics | "Your Instagram followers want a website to trust you more" | Areas with new residential growth |
| Physiotherapists | Mostly Instagram-only, very easy demo | Near hospitals, gyms |
| Young advocates | Building their practice, actively want clients | Near District Courts |
| Dietitians/nutritionists | Post-COVID boom, 100% Instagram-only | Anywhere |

**Target cities (Tier 2 sweet spot — less competition, good paying ability):**
1. **Ludhiana** — Start here. Punjab's largest city, same culture/language, far less agency competition than Chandigarh. Industrial base means people understand spending to earn.
2. **Jalandhar** — Sports/manufacturing exporters, NRI connections
3. **Indore** — Very business-forward, lots of SMEs
4. **Surat** — Rich trading community, pays without negotiating

**Skip:** Tier 1 metros (too competitive), Tier 3 towns (can't pay reliably)

### Phase 2 — Indian Diaspora Abroad (Month 3+)
Target Punjabi-owned businesses in UK, Canada, Australia, USA — restaurants, grocery stores, immigration consultants, tutoring centres. Same language, same culture = trust shortcut. Charge in their local currency.

| Country | Setup | Monthly | INR equivalent |
|---|---|---|---|
| UK | £200 | £60/month | ~6,400 INR |
| Canada | CAD 350 | CAD 80/month | ~4,800 INR |
| Australia | AUD 400 | AUD 90/month | ~4,900 INR |
| USA | $300 | $80/month | ~6,700 INR |

**Reach them via:** Punjabi community Facebook groups, Indian business WhatsApp groups abroad, Instagram (`#punjabibusiness london`, `#indianrestaurant toronto`)

## Tech Stack (zero cost)

```
Template      → Next.js or plain HTML/CSS (build once, reuse forever)
Hosting       → Vercel free tier (unlimited sites, custom domains, free SSL)
Content CMS   → Sanity free tier (already familiar from instaresume)
WhatsApp widget → Custom JS snippet (build once, drop on every site)
Domain        → Client buys on Namecheap (~₹300/year), you configure DNS
Payments      → Razorpay (already set up from instaresume)
```

**Why not FlowTrack/GHL yet:** Avoid monthly platform cost until you have 15-20 paying clients. Validate first, invest in platform later.

### Template Structure (5 sections)
1. **Hero** — Name, tagline, WhatsApp CTA button
2. **About** — Qualifications, experience, photo
3. **Services** — What they offer (3-6 items)
4. **Gallery** — Photos (pulled from their Instagram/Google Maps)
5. **Contact** — Address, phone, WhatsApp button, Google Maps embed

### WhatsApp Widget
Not just a link — build a proper widget:
- Pre-fills message: *"Hi, I found you on [website], I'd like to enquire about..."*
- Business hours awareness (shows call vs WhatsApp based on time)
- Click tracking (how many leads came from website)
- Floating button with custom branding

Build this once. It's your differentiator — no template-only agency can offer this.

## Outreach Process

### Finding clients (Google Maps method)
1. Search `"[vertical] [area] [city]"` on Google Maps
2. Click each result — businesses WITHOUT a "Website" button are your targets
3. Their phone number + photos are right there in the listing
4. Build demo using their real info before contacting them

### Demo-first pitch (the key to high conversion)
Build the demo BEFORE reaching out. Then:

**WhatsApp message (Hindi):**
```
"Sat Sri Akaal ji,

Maine Google Maps pe [Business Name] dekha.
Aapki website nahi hai toh humne ek sample
bana ke dekhi hai — 2 minute mein dekh sakte ho:

[demo link]

Pasand aaye toh aaj hi live kar dete hain —
2999 mein setup, 699/month hosting.
Ek patient ki fees mein poora saal covered."
```

**One-line pitch for professionals:**
*"When someone Googles your name, nothing professional comes up. We fix that today — 2999 once, 699/month."*

### Intern outreach model
- Interns find businesses on Google Maps (2 hours)
- Send WhatsApp messages (1 hour)
- Forward interested replies to you
- You share demo, handle payment
- Interns earn ₹500 per closed deal
